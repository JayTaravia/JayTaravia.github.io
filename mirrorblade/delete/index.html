Delete your profile & data

You can request deletion without reinstalling MIRRORBLADE.

Send a deletion request

Email jaytaravia7@gmail.com with the subject MIRRORBLADE deletion request. Include your player ID if you have it. If you do not, tell us your leaderboard alias and anything else that can help locate your profile. Never send passwords or a sign-in token.

Email a deletion request

You can also request deletion in the game under Settings → Privacy & data. Your player ID is shown there.

What happens next

Jay Taravia will review the request and may ask for proportionate proof of ownership. We aim to fulfill verified requests within 30 days, or sooner if required by law. We will confirm completion by email where you provided an address.

Deletion covers your MIRRORBLADE leaderboard profile and associated personal data. Your ranking may remain while processing finishes. We coordinate with PlayFab to remove relevant data and explain any necessary security, fraud-prevention, legal or backup retention. Because the backend also serves another game, we verify scope before deleting shared records.

Data independently processed by Google for advertising remains subject to Google's privacy controls. Contact us for help with a provider-related request.

Local progress

Uninstalling or clearing Android app storage removes local progress, settings and temporary cosmetic passes; it does not by itself delete your online profile. Save your player ID or contact support before removing app data.

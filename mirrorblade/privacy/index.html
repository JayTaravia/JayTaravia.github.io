Privacy policy

MIRRORBLADE by Jay Taravia

Last updated: 2026-09-29

Your game, on your device

Scores, settings, saved runs, collection items and temporary cosmetic passes are stored on your device. Core gameplay works offline. Clearing app data or uninstalling can remove this progress. There is no cross-device cloud save in the Android app.

Age selection

On first launch, we ask for your birth year to choose an age-appropriate experience. The year itself is discarded; only an adult or restricted category is stored locally. Under-18 and unknown profiles use offline play without ads or PlayFab leaderboard requests. The game is intended for teens and adults, not children under 13.

Optional online leaderboard

If an eligible player chooses Join leaderboard, Microsoft PlayFab creates a device-bound guest profile. PlayFab processes a random installation identifier, a player identifier, highest Endless score and service/network information such as IP address. A generated alias and highest score appear in the public top ten and your ranking. We do not ask for a real name, email, password, contacts or precise location to play. Keep your player ID when contacting support; reinstalling does not reliably recover a guest profile.

Advertising and privacy choices

Eligible adult profiles may see Google AdMob interstitials at completed-game breaks, spaced by at least 150 seconds, and optional rewarded ads. A completed rewarded ad unlocks the selected cosmetic for 24 hours. Ads are never required to continue core gameplay. Google Mobile Ads and the User Messaging Platform may process IP-derived approximate location, advertising/app/device identifiers, app and ad interactions, diagnostics and consent preferences for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Google test ads also use these SDKs. Where required, a consent form appears before eligible ad requests. You can revisit available choices in Settings > Ad privacy choices. Google may serve limited ads depending on your choices and region; refusing personalization is not always equivalent to refusing all ads.

Service providers and security

We use Microsoft PlayFab for rankings and Google AdMob/UMP for advertising and consent. Network requests use HTTPS. These providers may process data outside your country under their privacy terms. No Firebase Analytics or separate game analytics SDK is included. This website uses no developer-added analytics or advertising cookies; its hosting provider may process technical access logs to deliver and secure the pages.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until you remove it. Online leaderboard profile data remains until deletion is fulfilled. Request profile and associated-data deletion from Settings > Privacy & data or email jaytaravia7@gmail.com without reinstalling the game. Include your player ID if available; if not, describe the alias and any other information that helps locate your profile. We may ask for proportionate proof of ownership and aim to fulfill verified requests within 30 days, or a shorter period where law requires. Requests pause further score uploads on the requesting installation. Rankings may remain visible while the request is processed. A local reset alone does not delete remote data. Necessary security, fraud-prevention, legal records and service-provider backups may be retained for their applicable retention periods; we will explain any relevant exception when responding. Google advertising data is governed by Google's retention and privacy controls.

Your choices and contact

You can play offline, leave the leaderboard, change available ad privacy choices, request deletion, and ask to access or correct your data. Reset or delete your advertising ID through Android settings. Contact Jay Taravia at jaytaravia7@gmail.com for support or privacy requests. Depending on your location, you may also complain to your local data-protection authority. We will update this page and its date when our practices change.
